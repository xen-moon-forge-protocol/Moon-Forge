# Known issues and disclosures (public register)

Last updated 2026-10-22; the plan column of OPS-4 revised 2026-10-24 against the source of the final upgrade, and the wording of RULES-5 aligned with WORDING §2 the same day. This is the list of what is known to be wrong, missing, risky or still v2-only in Moon Forge **today**, each with a unique id. Ids are never reused or renumbered; a closed row stays here with its closing date and the commit or transaction that closed it. The labels H1, M1, L2 and so on in [REVIEW-2026-10.md](REVIEW-2026-10.md) are that document's own numbering of items fixed before deployment; they are a different series and are not referenced by bare label anywhere else. Four rows below (GAM-M3, GAM-L1, GAM-L3, GAM-X151) keep the labels of the internal games review (2026-10, not independent) under which the site's game panels list them; the panels and this register use the same ids.

How to read the dates: the program and the oracle live today are **v2** (program tag `program-2026-10-12`). A new oracle rule set (v3) and a final program upgrade are decided but **not live**. "At the switch" means the first epoch published under the v3 rules (date announced at least one full epoch ahead, together with the transaction of the last v2 publish). "At the final upgrade" means the program upgrade planned for November 2026, announced at least 7 days before it is deployed; if it is not deployed by 2026-12-31 the upgrade authority is burned on the then-current binary with the games delisted ([IMMUTABILITY.md](../IMMUTABILITY.md#when-the-upgrade-authority-will-be-burned)). Nothing listed as planned is a promise of a date; it is the plan as of the date above.

If GitHub (the repository, its Actions runners or GitHub Pages) becomes unavailable, the site and the weekly publication pause; the pool stays locked in the program, and every claim, withdrawal, recycle and settlement that is already determined keeps working from any client that can reach the chain.

## Games

| Id | What is wrong today | What it means for you | Plan (not live) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **GAM-1** | Every random outcome (house bets, Jackpot, Burn Lottery, the Moon Wars shuffle and, under v2, Forge Drop seeds) uses the hash of a future slot. The producer of that slot can influence or withhold it. Because the first entrant sets a round's end time, a validator can line a draw up with its own leader window, and the Genesis airdrop list is the validator set. X1 has no VRF. | Treat every game as a game of chance with this extra edge for whoever produces the target slot. House-game exposure is capped on-chain (max net win 0.25% of the free bankroll, unsettled payouts ≤ 20%, daily breaker at −10%); Jackpot and Burn Lottery pots are not capped this way. Stake only what you can lose. | The final upgrade moves draws to a keeper-committed hash chain with refunds on a stall. Until then the affected entry points stay live with this notice here and, from the site deploy of 2026-10-23, on the site. |
| **GAM-2** | Be the House quotes move with pending bets: deposits are priced as if every pending bet loses, withdrawals as if every pending bet wins, and the reserve is released only on settlement. A large bet opened just before your withdrawal lowers your quote. From the site deploy of 2026-10-23 the site blocks deposits and withdrawals while open bets reserve more than 1% of the bankroll (unless you override), and the kit's `houseDeposit` / `houseWithdraw` refuse in the same case unless forced, with a default minimum of 99% of the unreserved quote; the program itself still prices this way. | Before withdrawing, check that nothing is reserved (`bankroll_reserved` on the Transparency page, `status()` in the kit) and set your own minimum; wait for settlement rather than accepting a low quote. | Final upgrade: a 24-hour withdrawal window priced at settlement. |
| **GAM-3** | Predictions: a single entry can fill one side's pot cap and force a refund of the round (free blocking); the XDEX pool that sets the TWAP is thin, so a ~1% move is cheap; consecutive rounds share a price read; late entries have an edge. The earlier claim that manipulation "costs more than it can win" was wrong. | **Predictions are not offered by this project** (decision 2026-10-22): the site tab is removed with the site deploy of 2026-10-23 and the kit example is removed in the same release. The v2 instructions stay callable on-chain by anyone until the final upgrade; claims and refunds of past rounds keep working. Do not enter rounds. | Final upgrade: a replacement exists in the program only (per-side cap, several spaced price reads, a no-contest band, refund on any read failure); no site or kit entry path, only claim and refund exits. |
| **GAM-4** | Moon Wars: the creator always moves first (about 58/42 between equal greedy policies); an abandoned offer can cost the creator the stake on timeout once the keeper starts it; an expired start re-commits a new seed slot, so waiting re-rolls the shuffle; a player can stall for up to about 55 minutes. | Join rather than create if you can; do not open an offer you will not play; accept that the shuffle can be re-rolled by a slow start. | Final upgrade: sealed matches with slot-plus-seconds deadlines, first-mover balanced to 50 ± 2%, no re-roll. |
| **GAM-5** | Moon Wars on the site: the browser move key that pays a player's move fees was funded with about 0.005 XNT (two moves), so a real match could be lost on time after the second move. | **Creating and joining real-XNT matches is paused on the site since 2026-10-19** (commit `c9489df`). Existing matches can still be played, cancelled, claimed on timeout and paid out. Agents using the kit sign with their own keypair and are not affected. The free practice is unchanged. | Final upgrade: the wallet pays move fees and the leftover is refunded; the site reopens real matches only after a keeper has met the published upkeep deadlines for a week. |
| **GAM-6** | Artifact Duel: the joiner's element is sent in plain text and the creator can cancel after seeing it. | Join only stakes you are willing to see cancelled; the creator cannot win by this, only avoid losing. | Final upgrade: both sides commit; cancel refused after a commit. |
| **GAM-7** | A house bet must be settled within about 3 minutes (the SlotHashes window) or it counts as lost even if the roll won. The site settles immediately, but switching to a wallet app or locking the screen on a phone can suspend the page, and the public keeper cannot be relied on for this window (OPS-1). | Bet from a desktop browser that stays open, or accept the risk; keep stakes small on mobile. From the site deploy of 2026-10-23 the site warns about this before a bet on a phone. | Final upgrade: refund instead of loss when a bet is not settled in time. |
| **GAM-8** | Burn Lottery: wagering credits chips (about 0.2 XNT of play per chip, against 1 XNT tickets), so chip farmers dilute the prize; a round with a single entrant pays 50% of the vault for one chip. | The expected value of a ticket depends on how many chips are in the round; assume it is below the ticket price. | Planned for the final upgrade: chips from every source set to zero and new Burn Lottery tickets retired (the lottery's share of rakes goes to the reward pool instead); rounds already open are settled or refunded under their own rules. |
| **GAM-M3** | Be the House: a matured withdrawal request never expires. A depositor can hold an unlocked request indefinitely and leave the moment the bankroll turns against the others (in the games review's two-day drain scenario the armed depositor left at −2% while another lost −17%). | The depositors who stay carry the loss the armed one leaves behind; the bankroll's result can turn against you faster than the 24-hour cooldown lets you exit. | Final upgrade: a matured request must be used within 24 hours, then renewed. |
| **GAM-L1** | The daily circuit breaker checks the bankroll before open bets are counted, so a day can end worse than −10% when several large open bets win at once. | The −10% daily bound on house losses is approximate while bets are open (the per-bet and aggregate caps still hold). | Final upgrade: the breaker counts open bets as won. |
| **GAM-L3** | The public keeper settles at most 60 bets per pass, in no particular order, so a flood of tiny bets can let someone else's winning bet expire unsettled (see GAM-7). | Settle your own bet: the site does it right after the next slot; keep the page open until the result shows. | Final upgrade: winners first, oldest first, in batches. |
| **GAM-X151** | Disclosure: a house bet nobody settles within about 3 minutes counts as lost even on a winning roll, and its stake stays in the bankroll. Depositors, the project's own seed deposit included, gain from every missed settlement, and the project also runs the fallback keeper. | A conflict of interest that the program does not remove today; it is bounded by settling your own bet (GAM-7). | Final upgrade: unsettled bets are refunded instead of counted as lost. |

## Operations and dependencies

| Id | What is wrong today | What it means for you | Plan (not live) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **OPS-1** | All automation runs on GitHub Actions cron: the keeper is scheduled every 15 minutes, but GitHub fires only a share of those ticks (in practice a run every few hours); the oracle is scheduled Sundays 00:00 UTC and can be late; the health check runs once a day. Upkeep with a window (bet settlement, draw and lottery settlement, Forge Drop seals within about 512 slots, Moon Wars starts) can be missed, and claims submitted on your behalf can come hours late. | Claim your own leaf from Mission Control instead of waiting; settle your own bets; anyone can run `npm run keeper` with a funded key and is tipped by the pool. An unsettled draw is refunded or forfeited to the pool, never re-rolled. | A dispatch trigger outside GitHub cron, a public status feed with the last keeper and oracle run, and a keeper service level with alerts. |
| **OPS-2** | The site and the kit use one X1 RPC (`rpc.mainnet.x1.xyz`) and one public RPC per EVM chain. Rate limits or an outage show as data that will not load or transactions that will not send. | Funds are not affected by an RPC outage. Retry later, or use the kit with your own RPC. | Site and kit: a list of RPCs with failover and a user-settable RPC; a "could not load" state distinct from "nothing to show". |
| **OPS-3** | The site, the epoch files and this documentation are served from GitHub Pages and the repository. If the GitHub account or Pages becomes unavailable, the site and the weekly publication stop. | The pool stays locked in the program; claims of an epoch that is already published, withdrawals, recycles and settlements keep working from any client with the IDL (the kit, or a copy of the site). Burns made during a stop are read when publication resumes; if publication never resumes they are not paid. | No Arweave copy of the epoch files (revised 2026-10-25; this row first planned one): every clone of the repository and the oracle run's 90-day artifact are the copies, each checked against the data hash stored on-chain; a documentation manifest at the freeze; the dependence is disclosed, not removed. |
| **OPS-4** | The oracle key is a hot key (a repository secret) held by one anonymous operator; the architect/upgrade key is held by the same anonymous operator outside GitHub, used only for the announced final upgrade and the freeze, and kept offline from the final upgrade to the freeze ([SECURITY.md, Custody](../SECURITY.md#upgrade-authority)). The program bounds what the oracle can do (≤ 10% of the free pool per epoch, ≥ 6 days apart) but cannot replace a lost or stolen oracle key on its own today. | Read [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md) for the bounds. A stolen or lost oracle key is bounded to at most one epoch budget per 6 days; today only a program upgrade (while the authority exists) could replace it against the holder's will. | Final upgrade (its source as of 2026-10-24): every epoch claimable only 48 hours after its publication, and anyone can void a wrong one during that hold with a bond paid to the pool; the oracle key in an oracle seat whose offline cold key can replace a stolen hot key; if no epoch has survived its hold for 28 days, the largest voider can take the seat from day 28 and anyone from day 29, with a bond. No veto, heartbeat or pause instruction; the architect stays a payee wallet that can only move or renounce its own address. Documented in the upgrade announcement. |
| **OPS-5** | The v2 oracle builds each epoch from all five chains in one run: if a block range, the XEN market or a price read fails on any one chain, the whole build aborts (never skips blocks) and nothing is published by that run. | Nothing partial is ever written or paid. The next run inside the 48-hour publish window retries ([DEPLOY.md §4](../DEPLOY.md#4-oracle)); if no run succeeds in the window, nothing is published that week and the burns wait for the next epoch, valued by the same rules. Funds are not affected. | v3: a chain whose read fails is skipped with its cursor kept, and its burns rank after the next week's burns (disclosed in the epoch file). |
