# Claims register

Every trust, fairness or safety claim Moon Forge makes in public, with where it is made, the evidence anyone can check, and its status in the three tenses of [WORDING.md](WORDING.md): **today** (v2 program, tag `program-2026-10-12`; v2 oracle), **from the switch epoch** (v3 oracle rules, planned for November 2026 with the date announced at least one full epoch ahead; not live) and **after the final upgrade** (earliest 2026-11-11; the freeze earliest 2026-11-25, no later than 2026-12-31). Checked against the repository and the chain on 2026-10-22. This file is a release gate: a claim that is not here, or whose status is not TRUE for the text's tense, does not ship.

Status words: **TRUE** · **CAVEAT** (true with a stated limit that the text must carry) · **OVERSTATED** (true in part; the fix and its owner are named) · **FALSE** · **FUTURE** (true only after the dated event; publishable only as a dated notice) · **RETIRED** (will stop being true at the dated event).

Evidence shorthand: `constants.rs` = `programs/moon-forge/src/constants.rs`; `tests:N` = `tests/moon-forge.test.ts` line N (the local-validator suite, run by `bash tests/run-local.sh`); `epoch-1` = `frontend/public/epochs/epoch-1.json`; `index` = `frontend/public/epochs/index.json`; `portals` = `deployments/portals.json`.
