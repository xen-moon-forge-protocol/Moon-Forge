# Agent kit changelog

Dates are UTC. Versions follow semver; the repository tag of a release is `kit-v<version>`. Deprecation policy: a kit
version is deprecated when the agent card names a higher `minKitVersion`; `checkKitVersion(card)` (and
`npm run watch -- --card`) report it. Never auto-update a funded agent: pin the tag you reviewed.

## 2.0.0 (unreleased; tag `kit-v2.0.0` planned for the day of the final program upgrade, earliest 2026-11-11)

Everything below that needs the v3 rules or the upgraded program is switched on by what the chain reports and is
refused, with nothing sent, before that. Against the live program (tag `program-2026-10-12`) the kit sends the same
instructions as 1.0.0.

**F5, the carried liquidity rule (rules block 2, 2026-10-26), switched on with the v3 rules like everything below**
- `quote-v3.mjs` follows rules block 2 of the oracle engine (`RULES_ID_V3 = 2`). A chain valued in an epoch whose hourly
  liquidity feed is missing or thin (fewer than 4 recorded samples on more than 2 of the 7 days, or none at all; the
  build's own reading does not count) keeps its liquidity L for the next week (`L.nextRule` "carried"). Otherwise the
  next L moves within the clamp as before ("clamp"). An orphaned chain is never carried. `PRICE_RULES_V3.liquidity`
  gains `minSamplesPerDay` (4) and `maxThinDays` (2). A rules block, a previous epoch file or a week-so-far feed that
  names rulesId 1 (the round-4 block, replaced before any v3 epoch was built) is refused, so such a feed gives no quote.
  A quote does not change: the week's L in force is the one set at the previous build.
- The vectors (`test/vectors/quote-v3.json`) were regenerated by the engine under rulesId 2, with two new worlds that
  record a feed (`f5-carried-*`). `test/unit/f5-carried.test.mjs` covers the rule, and `test/unit/rules-vectors.test.mjs`
  also compares `L.nextRule`. `test/vectors/week-so-far.v1.json` is unchanged.

**Round 4 of the final upgrade (2026-10-25), switched on by the upgraded program and the v3 rules like everything below**
- The bundled upgraded IDL is the round-4 build (sha256 `9617cd5e…a72f`): `settle_bet_v2` also takes the reward vault and
  the registered keeper wallet, `refund_stalled_bet` reads the reward vault without writing it, `BetSettled` ends with
  `keeper_tip`.
- House bets: a stalled bet costs the keeper's bond. Anchored and not revealed in time, or skipped: the bond pays the
  payout and the bankroll keeps the stake as for a lost bet. Never anchored: the player gets the whole stake back (the
  bankroll returns the stake minus the edge, the bond pays the edge) and the bond also forfeits the stake minus the
  edge, which nobody receives (stallOutcome().bondForfeit), so no LP, burner or player gains from a bet left
  unanchored. `waitBet()` and
  `settledBetResult()` say so, and report the settle tip the keeper wallet received (`keeperTipXnt`, at most 0.0005 XNT,
  at most half the bet's 1% pool share). `rules-v3.mjs` mirrors `KEEPER_SETTLE_TIP`, `settleTip()` and `stallOutcome()`.
- `quote-v3.mjs` follows the round-4 rules of the oracle engine: the hashed rules block, the same-second order by
  keccak256(txHash ‖ logIndex), a skipped chain's backlog and the operator wallets ranked after the window's burns, the
  window's liquidity frozen at the previous build, the XDEX tranche cap, the USDC.X guard and the orphaned-chain price.
  The vectors (`test/vectors/quote-v3.json`) were regenerated with new worlds for each rule; the oracle's own round-4
  vectors are replayed too (`test/unit/rules-vectors.test.mjs`).
- `quoteBurn()`: the market route comes from the feed's `route` and `xdex` (the oracle writes them every hour), so a
  burn is checked against selling without `allowBelowMarket`; no quote for a chain whose liquidity is set only at the
  weekly build, or for an operator wallet outside a dated exception; the feed's `capacity.senior` counts the window's
  own burns. `verifyEpochFileOffline()` accepts a heartbeat epoch (no leaf, nothing paid). The seat sentence reads: the
  largest voider can take the oracle seat from day 28, anyone from day 29.

**Changed for every user**
- `connect()` uses the program interface (IDL) bundled with the kit (`frontend/src/idl/`, pinned by sha256 in
  `idl.mjs`) instead of reading the IDL account on chain, which its authority can change until the freeze. The live
  IDL is used while the program reports the current rules, the upgraded program's IDL once it reports the final
  upgrade. `connect({ idl: 'chain' })` keeps the old behaviour; `connect({ idl })` with an object or a path is used as
  given (its address is checked), except a path to a copy of a bundled file (such as the `frontend/src/idl/moon_forge.json`
  path the 1.0.0 README suggested for `MOONFORGE_IDL`), which behaves like the bundle and switches with the rules.
- The program rules are detected at `connect()` (`detect.mjs`, the oracle's own verdict on X1 mainnet: ProgramData
  last-deploy slot and the upgraded program's first accounts) and re-read every 10 minutes on mainnet. When the
  chain shows an unclear state (a half-done upgrade) every new transaction is refused until it is clear, re-read every
  15 s; a failed RPC read keeps the last clear reading and is retried within 15 s. A step of a sealed game whose own
  account proves the upgrade (seed, reveal, open, timeout) still goes ahead, and the moves of a running match are never
  held. On any other cluster the kit keeps the live rules unless `connect({ rules: 3 })` or `connect({ rules: 'detect' })`
  (so a local test harness written for the live calls keeps working). With your own endpoints, `connect()` refuses when
  none of them answers `getGenesisHash` (pass `genesis` or `rules`), and `rules: 3` on X1 mainnet is checked against
  the chain. RPC error texts in detection reasons are cut to the URL origin (a private endpoint's key never reaches a
  warning or `status()`).
- `connect({ card })`: with the agent card, every send is refused while this kit is older than its `minKitVersion`.
- Memcmp filter offsets and the `settle_bet_v2` discriminator are read from the IDL in use (`idl.mjs`), never hard-coded.
- `status()` adds `kitVersion`, `rules`, `oracle` (last publication, age, `fresh` / `due` / `late`) and `capacity`.
- Instructions without a measured compute-unit limit (all the new ones) are simulated first and sent with that
  measurement ×1.2; a refusal in the simulation is reported as the program error, with no fee spent.
- `send()` accepts `priority` (a compute-unit price, from `connect({ priorityFee })`) and `resend` (the same signed
  bytes rebroadcast every 2 s until confirmed: one signature, so it can only land once).
- The error map knows the final upgrade's codes 6064-6100 (`PROGRAM_ERRORS_V3`); an unknown code is now one above 6100.

**The burner's exits (from the v3 switch epoch; the claims work today)**
- `verifyMyClaim(epoch)`: the leaf, its proof, the root, the data hash, the total score and the leaf count of the
  program's Epoch account, then whether it can be claimed now (`verify-leaf.mjs`, shared conventions).
- `claim(epoch)`, `claimFor(owner, epoch)`, `claimAll()`: the XNT always goes to the wallet in the leaf.
- `myMissions()`, `withdrawVested(mission)`, `withdrawAllVested()`, `eject(mission, { acceptPenalty: true })`.
- `donate('reward' | 'bankroll', xnt)`, `claimAirdrop({ list })` (the Genesis claim, checked against the stored root).
- `myBurns()`, `myLeaves()`, `history()` (CSV: bets, matches, duels, forges, sales, claims, Be the House deposits and
  withdrawals, vested withdrawals, ejects, Jackpot, Lottery and drop results), `capacity()`.
- `closeJackpotEntry(round, index)`, `closeDrawEntry(round, index)`: close an entry of a settled round (a refunded
  Jackpot round credits the stake back); the rent returns to whoever paid it. Work under both rules.
- Epoch files come from `connect({ epochs })` (a directory, a mirror URL, or `'site'`); the kit fetches nothing over
  HTTPS unless you configure it.
- `examples/verify-epoch.mjs` (`npm run verify-epoch`): every published file against its on-chain account.
- `examples/watch.mjs` (`npm run watch`): a read-only dead-man watch (late oracle, rules changed, deploy, kit outdated,
  low wallet, unreadable chain).

**The v3 burn module (`burn.mjs`; fails closed until the switch epoch)**
- `quoteBurn()`: the binding quote of the v3 rules, computed by a line-by-line port of the oracle engine
  (`quote-v3.mjs`, checked against golden vectors the oracle engine produced, with a sha256 checksum), from the
  week-so-far feed the v3 oracle writes. Refusals: no quote (fail closed, no override), a skipped chain, the market
  route above the quote (`allowBelowMarket`), beyond today's capacity (`allowBeyondCapacity`), more than half of what
  is left of today's tranche (`allowLargeShare`), a late oracle (without `oracle`, from the feed's window start), a
  pending recovery, a quarantined chain (`allowStaleOracle`), vesting tiers (`allowVesting`), the last minutes of an
  hour (`allowHourEdge`); and, with no override, a feed that does not continue epoch n − 1, a feed whose derived
  fields disagree with its raw burns, and a local clock behind a burn already in the feed.
- One week-so-far feed schema for the kit and the site (the FEED CONTRACT in `burn.mjs`: the raw window plus the
  derived fields the site reads; `normalizeFeed()`), with an example `test/vectors/week-so-far.v1.json`
  (`npm run vectors:feed`).
- `burnXen()`: with an ethers v6 Signer you bring and an X1 connection (`x1Connection`, required: the destination and
  the oracle's last publication are read on X1); approves the exact amount (never unlimited), re-quotes before
  `enterForge`, handles `TRANSACTION_REPLACED` (repriced counts, cancelled does not) and checks the `MissionStarted`
  event; the destination is confirmed by its last 6 characters.
- `checkX1Destination()`, `x1ToBytes32()`, `bytes32ToX1()`, `destinationPreview()`, `planBurns()` (pacing; only its
  first piece is binding).

**The final upgrade (after the upgrade transaction; refused before it)**
- House games on the keeper's hash chain: `placeBetV2()` (anchored right after placing), `anchorBet()`, `betStatus()`,
  `waitBet()` (with the roll checked against the keeper's reveal), `refundStalledBet()`, `houseKeeper()`.
  `placeBet()` / `settleBet()` of keeper bets refuse with `KeeperRequired`.
- Sealed Artifact Duel: `createDuelSealed()`, `joinDuelSealed()`, `revealDuelJoin()`, `revealDuel()`,
  `claimDuelTimeout()`, `cancelDuel()`, `openDuels()`, `myDuels()`. Sealed Moon Wars: `createWarSealed()`,
  `joinWarSealed()`, `advanceStart()`; `startWar()` and `playMatch()` handle both kinds of match. `createWar()` /
  `joinWar()` refuse with `SealedJoinRequired`. No Artifact is needed for either game.
- The secrets of sealed games are written to `~/.moonforge/secrets/<program>/` (mode 0600, fsynced) before the
  transaction that commits to them, and reused on a retry (`secrets.mjs`; `connect({ secretsDir })`). A record is
  published without replacing an existing one: two processes with the same key creating the same game both get the
  first record written. `createDuelSealed()` / `joinDuelSealed()` never return the sealed element.
- Artifacts: `forgePrice(tier)`, `forge(tier, { maxPriceXnt })` (PX price), `artifactInfo()`,
  `isGenuineArtifact()`, `transferArtifact()` (a Core transfer; works under both rules), the market (`listings()`,
  `listArtifact()`, `updateListing()`, `delistArtifact()`, `closeListing()`, `buyArtifact()`), the extra pot
  (`verifyExtraClaim()`, `claimExtra()`, `claimAllExtra()`). `forgeLunarDust()` forges at the PX price with its
  10 XNT maximum.
- `houseStats()` (share value, haircut, the daily breaker, the weekly limit, the keeper; the realised 7- and 30-day
  change from your own snapshots, never a rate), `recordHouseSnapshot()`.
- `checkKitVersion(card)`, `fetchAgentCard()` (explicit HTTPS fetch), `KIT_VERSION`.

**Not in this kit**: no entry into Predictions (the kit only advances and claims rounds), no session-key mode, no
"snipe the tranche" example, no `seal_drop`, no rescue command line or single-file page yet, no `metadata(asset)`
gateway fallback, no `ascend`. The agent key stays separate from any treasury key.
